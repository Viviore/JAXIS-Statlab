## 2. Step-by-Step Screen Guide

### Screen 1: The CEO Executive Console (`/dashboard/ceo`)

When you log in, your main screen displays the 4 core health cards of JAXIS StatLab:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ JAXIS EXECUTIVE CONSOLE                                                 │
│                                                                         │
│ ┌───────────────────┐ ┌───────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ GROSS REVENUE     │ │ ESCROW HOLDINGS   │ │ ACTIVE RESEARCH STUDIES │ │
│ │ ₱348,500          │ │ ₱142,000          │ │ 28 Studies Running      │ │
│ │ This month's sales│ │ Safe client funds │ │ Zero overdue deadlines  │ │
│ └───────────────────┘ └───────────────────┘ └─────────────────────────┘ │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Gross Revenue:** Total cash received this month from clients.
- **Escrow Holdings:** Total money currently protected in our safe vault while statisticians are actively working.
- **Active Studies:** Total number of theses and dissertations currently underway across all specialists.

---

### Screen 2: Checking Team Workload & Hiring Needs (`Staff`)

1. Click **"Staff Management"** in your sidebar.
2. Review your specialists:
   - If most statisticians are at **4/4 studies** (maximum capacity): That means demand is booming! Time to onboard another qualified biostatistician or SPSS analyst.
   - If specialists have 0 or 1 active study: You have spare capacity to run a promotion or market to graduate schools.

---

### Screen 3: Setting Pay Rates & Commission Rules (`Settings`)

1. Go to **"Payroll Settings"** in the sidebar.
2. You have the authority to configure the team compensation rules:
   - **Monthly Base Retainer:** Guaranteed monthly base pay for loyal team members.
   - **Hourly Duty Rate:** Hourly compensation paid for logged work hours on the topbar timer.
   - **Study Commission Split:** By default, specialists earn **60%** of the project package fee, QA earns **15%**, and company margin is **25%**. You can adjust this or give custom bonuses to senior specialists handling complex medical PhD papers.

---

### Screen 4: Final Decisions on Client Disputes (`Disputes`)

If a student has a disagreement with their adviser's panel or claims the statistical method was not what they wanted:
1. Operations Admin reviews it first.
2. If a full refund exceeding ₱10,000 is requested, it comes to your desk.
3. You can review the agreement, read the messages between the student and analyst, and make the executive call:
   - Authorize a free re-audit by our Senior QA Lead.
   - Or approve an escrow refund to protect the student.

---

## 3. Executive Directives

1. **Reputation Over Quick Profit:** Never let a rush project compromise our quality. One student passing their thesis defense leads to five of their classmates enrolling with us!
2. **Never Fabricate Data:** If a client ever asks to make up fake numbers to pass their defense, support the team in rejecting and refunding the study. JAXIS stands for honest science.
3. **Take Care of the Team:** Pay specialists and QA leads on time every 15th and 30th. Great analysts make JAXIS the best in the industry.
