## 3. General Rules Everyone Follows

1. **Keep All Chat Inside JAXIS:** Never ask for or give personal phone numbers, Facebook accounts, personal Telegrams, or private GCash numbers. Keeping all messages inside JAXIS protects your money, protects your work, and ensures our quality checkers can review what happened if a question arises.
2. **Honest Science Only:** We compute real, honest math. We never make up fake numbers, fake survey answers, or forge results.
3. **Safe Escrow Protection:** Clients never lose money to someone who disappears, and specialists never work for free. Money is deposited safely upfront, held securely, and released only when the quality check is 100% passed.
